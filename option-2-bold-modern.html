Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be “fit” to start?

No. Every package starts with an assessment, and your program will meet you where you are and evolve as you get stronger. Many clients start as beginners.

How do consultations work?

The work begins with a free initial consultation. We’ll talk through your goals, identify the places you’ve gotten stuck before, and make a plan so you not only hit your goals but maintain them.

What’s included in a package?

Every package includes a comprehensive movement assessment, a personalized nutrition report, access to your coaching app, a habit-setting session, and ongoing accountability and support.

Do you offer online training?

Yes—self-paced online coaching starts at $180/mo with 2–4 personalized weekly workouts, a monthly virtual progress check-in, and unlimited messaging.
