Strength, on your terms

Strength—physically, mentally, and emotionally

I help people build strength—physically, mentally, and emotionally. My training approach is based on creating positive, empowering experiences in the gym, especially for those who’ve felt unwelcome, judged, or defeated by fitness culture in the past.

Personal training

What sets my personal training apart?

Workouts that work for you

For real results, you need an expertly designed program. You’ll get a safe, smart, and progressive strength training program, designed with my PhD-level knowledge of exercise science principles.

Tailored programs

Every personal training package starts with an assessment. We’ll discuss your goals and do posture, movement, and strength tests. Your program will meet you where you are and evolve as you get stronger.

Mindful coaching

Beyond sets and reps, you’ll be guided through each exercise with intention. You’ll learn how to activate your core, use perfect form for each movement, and build a strong connection between your body and mind.

Progress tracking

Progression is the key to getting results from strength training. Every exercise you do will be carefully tracked so we can see and celebrate your progress as you conquer physical challenges.

Habits that support your goals

We’ll work on small but powerful lifestyle habits that can make a big difference in your overall health and well-being and help you achieve the fulfilling and meaningful life you want.

Support and accountability

Change can be hard, but you don’t have to do it alone. I’ll be there to provide encouragement and motivation. I truly care about your success and I’ll believe in you until you believe in yourself.

My methodology

A whole-person approach

Whole-Person Coaching

A healthy lifestyle should make your life better, not take anything away from it. Together we’ll create a program that supports your goals and that feels balanced and achievable for you.

Client-Centered

You guide the process. You know your habits, your obstacles, your mindset, and what you’re willing and able to do. I’m here to help you keep the habits that serve you and ditch the ones that don’t.

Habits-Based

Starting where you are and taking small steps in the right direction. Turn small, achievable changes into habits, then build on them—that’s what sticks.
