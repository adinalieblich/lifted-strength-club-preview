About the studio

About Lifted Strength Club

Lifted Strength Club is a semi-private strength training studio in Longwood, FL, built for people who want to get stronger in body and mind. Our classes are small, coach-led and beginner-friendly — never intimidating. Whether you’re new to lifting or returning after years away, you’ll train with real, personalized attention in a welcoming community.

Our philosophy is anti-shame and science-led: no weigh-ins, no before-and-after photos, no pressure to “transform.” We believe strength is for your physical and mental health — and we coach it that way.

The coach behind Lifted

Science, lived experience & a refusal to do it the shallow way

I struggled for many years with depression and anxiety, and what changed my life for the better was running and strength training. Through exercise I gained self-confidence, resilience, patience — and genuinely felt better in my own skin.

After 19 years of training clients and studying exercise science, I have the tools to help you build the rewarding life you want. I’ve supported 300+ clients as a personal trainer and health coach.

“You can get a personal training certification in a weekend. I’m not that kind of trainer — I’ve dedicated decades to the art and science of exercise.”

My approach integrates motivation science and behavioural psychology to help you make lifestyle changes you can actually stick with — no weigh-ins, no before-and-after photos, no pressure to “transform.”
