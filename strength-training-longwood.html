Our flagship — semi-private strength training

A welcoming place to get strong — right here in Longwood

Lifted Strength Club is a semi-private strength training studio at 2100 N Ronald Reagan Blvd #1012, Longwood, FL 32750. Classes are intentionally small (no more than 5 people) and led by a certified coach, so whether you’re brand new to lifting or returning after years away, you get real, personalized attention in a friendly, judgement-free space.

It’s strength training for both your body and your mind — built around confidence, resilience, energy, healthy ageing and feeling genuinely better, not intimidation or extreme fitness culture.
