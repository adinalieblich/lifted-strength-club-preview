Class Schedule

When we train

Lifted runs coach-led, semi-private strength classes — 55 minutes, capped at five people. Rather than dropping into a different class each time, members claim one fixed weekly slot at enrolment and train at the same time each week, so you build strength alongside the same small, familiar group.

How scheduling works

Claim your weekly slot

Classes aren't booked one at a time. At your consultation we find the time that fits your week, reserve your place in a small group, and that becomes your standing session — same day, same time, every week.
