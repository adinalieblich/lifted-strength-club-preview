01 · Logo

The mark

The primary logo is the stacked mark — a gold sunrise rising over a barbell, with the wordmark in Outfit. It reverses to cream on dark and teal on light. Never recolour, stretch, or rearrange it.

Emblem / favicon — 64 · 40 · 24 px
Left: the standalone emblem (app icon, favicon, embroidery, social avatar). Right: the horizontal lockup — for headers, signage and email signatures.
Clear spaceKeep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the L in LIFTED.
Minimum sizeStacked mark no smaller than 120px / 32mm wide. Below that, use the emblem alone.
DontsNo gradients on the wordmark, no recolouring the structure, no outlines, no busy photo without a scrim.
02 · Colour

A palette of strength & calm

Teal is the primary ink. Brass and blush are accents — brass for premium touches, blush for human warmth. Warm greige is the default background; charcoal is text. The gold in the sunrise is drawn from the brass family.

03 · Typography

Two typefaces

Cormorant Garamond carries the headlines — elegant, editorial, premium. Outfit handles everything else: body copy, UI, labels, and the logo wordmark — warm, clean, legible at any size.
