My methodology

A whole-person approach

1

Whole-Person Coaching

A healthy lifestyle should make your life better, not take anything away from it. We’ll create a program that feels balanced and achievable for you.

2

Client-Centered

You guide the process. You’re the expert in your life—I’m here to help you keep the habits that serve you and ditch the ones that don’t.

I struggled for many years with depression and anxiety, and what changed my life for the better was running and strength training. Through exercise I gained self-confidence, resilience, patience, and just genuinely felt better in my own skin.

After more than 18 years of training clients and studying exercise science, I have the tools to help you build the rewarding life you want.
