Our flagship — semi-private strength training

Semi-Private Personal Training

Our semi-private personal training classes combine personalized coaching with the energy and support of a small community. Every workout is guided by a coach and designed to help you build strength safely and confidently, whether you’re brand new to strength training or returning after years away from exercise.

This is not an intimidating gym environment or a bootcamp-style workout. Classes are intentionally small (no more than 5 people), so you receive personalized attention, learn proper form, and progress at your own pace in a welcoming, supportive space.

Who it’s for:

Beginners who don’t know where to startAnyone who feels uncomfortable in traditional gymsBusy adults who want structure & accountabilityGetting stronger, physically and mentally

How it works

1

Consultation

We begin with a conversation about your goals, fitness history, injuries and experience level. We meet you where you are and help you use exercise to live the life you want.

2

Onboarding

Two one-on-one sessions with a certified trainer: a full movement assessment, your personalized program built into your workout app, and coaching on the fundamentals so you feel confident from day one.

3

Join your group

In 55-minute coach-led sessions you follow your personalized app-guided program. A trainer leads the group of no more than 5, helping with form, spotting, weight selection and motivation.
